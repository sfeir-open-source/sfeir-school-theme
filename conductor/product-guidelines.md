# Product Guidelines

## Communication Style
- **Direct & Technical:** Documentation and CLI output must prioritize precision and efficiency. Use standard industry terminology and provide clear, actionable information without unnecessary verbosity.

## Visual Identity & Accessibility
- **High-Contrast Optimization:** The theme must prioritize high-contrast color pairings and bold typography. This ensures maximum readability on diverse output devices, especially lower-quality projectors common in training environments.
- **Sharp Assets:** All brand logos and icons should be handled in vector formats (SVG) or high-resolution web formats to maintain a professional look across all display types.

## Branding Strategy
- **Discreet Presence:** When branding is active, it must remain subtle and non-intrusive (e.g., footer logos, corner placement). The focus must always be on the trainer's educational content.
- **Configurable Branding:** The display of brand elements is toggled via project parameters. This allows the theme to adapt to different contexts, from fully branded SFEIR training to a generic, unbranded presentation mode.
- **Content-First:** Educational material takes precedence over brand aesthetics. Layouts should be designed to maximize the space available for technical information and code.
