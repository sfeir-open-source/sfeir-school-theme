# Initial Concept

The project goal is to provide an Open Source theme for RevealJS presentations that respects the graphical identity of SFEIR, specifically for use in SFEIR School and SFEIR Institute training programs.

# Product Guide

## Target Audience
The primary audience consists of SFEIR trainers and speakers. They require a consistent, professional, and easily deployable slide theme that aligns with SFEIR's brand identity while providing a smooth experience for technical presentations.

## Core Goals
- **Consistency:** Ensure all SFEIR training materials have a unified look and feel.
- **Ease of Use:** Provide a theme that is easy to integrate into existing RevealJS workflows.
- **Maintainability:** Offer tools (CLI) that help trainers keep their slide decks up to date with the latest theme changes.
- **Developer Focus:** Prioritize technical content readability, especially code snippets.

## Key Features
- **RevealJS & TalkControl Integration:** Full compatibility with the RevealJS ecosystem and specialized extensions for speaker notes, multi-column layouts, and more.
- **Branding Modes:** Automatic switching between "School" (Green), "Institute" (Blue), and "Conf" modes to adapt to different training contexts.
- **Maintenance CLI:** A dedicated CLI to manage project cleanup, version migration, and initial setup.
- **KISS UI:** Keep the tool UI simple and focused on the presentation itself, avoiding unnecessary complexity for the trainer.

## Design Philosophy
- **Professional & Corporate:** Polished aesthetic suitable for professional training environments.
- **Developer-Centric:** Optimized for code legibility and technical clarity.
- **Educational & Clear:** High contrast and specific layouts (e.g., exercise slides, blur slides) to facilitate learning and engagement.
